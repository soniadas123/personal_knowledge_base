# My Publication

**Summary**: Links to my own publications.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

- [A Geospatial Study of Archaeological Remains at Halebidu: An Integrative Approach to Identify Unexplored Features](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12524-020-01272-8): Das, S. & Rajani, M.B., *Journal of the Indian Society of Remote Sensing* 49(5), 1025–1034 (2021). Uses remote sensing and GIS to identify previously unrecorded archaeological features around the Halebidu heritage site. See also [[Archaeology]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: Halebidu, remote sensing, GIS, archaeology, heritage mapping, unexplored features
- [Radiocarbon Dating of Historical Bricks: Exploring the Unprotected Archaeological Mounds in the Environs of Excavated Site of Nalanda](https://insa.nic.in/writereaddata/UpLoadedFiles/PINSA/PINSA_2019_Art27.pdf): Kumar, P., Das, S., Rajani, M.B. & Chopra, S., *Proceedings of the Indian National Science Academy* 85(3), 619–628 (2019). Applies radiocarbon dating to bricks from unprotected mounds near Nalanda to assess their antiquity relative to the excavated and protected World Heritage site. See also [[Archaeology]]. Keywords: radiocarbon dating, Nalanda, archaeological mounds, heritage protection, brick dating, PINSA
- [Archaeological Exploration in Srirangapatna and Its Environ Through Remote Sensing Analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12524-017-0659-9): Gupta, E., Das, S. & Rajani, M.B., *Journal of the Indian Society of Remote Sensing* 45(6), 1057–1063 (2017). Uses high-resolution multispectral imagery and Cartosat stereo pairs on a GIS platform to identify archaeological structures in and around Srirangapatna fort. See also [[Archaeology]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: Srirangapatna, remote sensing, GIS, Cartosat, fort archaeology, stereo imagery
- [The Need for a National Archaeological Database](https://www.jstor.org/stable/26494845): Gupta, E., Das, S., Balan, K.S.C., Kumar, V. & Rajani, M.B., *Current Science*, 1961–1973 (2017). Argues for a centralized, standardized national database of archaeological sites in India to support research, conservation, and heritage protection. See also [[Archaeology]], [[Data]]. Keywords: archaeological database, heritage data, India, conservation, data standardization, Current Science
- [Archaeological Remains in and Around Avati Hill](https://ijarch.com/ija-vol-2-number-3/): Das, S., Gupta, E. & Rajani, M.B., *Indian Journal of Archaeology* 2(3), 36–47 (2017). Uses satellite imagery and fieldwork to map heritage remains near Avati hill (~40 km north of Bangalore) associated with the Nadaprabhu rulers and prehistoric occupation, threatened by modern development. See also [[Archaeology]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: Avati hill, Nadaprabhu, Bangalore, satellite imagery, heritage mapping, prehistoric remains

# Urban Water and Resilience

**Summary**: Notes on urban water management, water resilience, and related infrastructure and climate adaptation.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

- [Building a Resilient Bengaluru — Resources & Final Report](https://buildingaresilientbengaluru.com/resources/#city-report): Resources page for Building a Resilient Bengaluru, a public information campaign for stormwater resilience in Bengaluru, co-authored with Shravya Ponnaluri, Namoshi Basu, and Aliza under the Bengaluru Sustainability Forum's Small Grants Programme (2025–26). Includes the campaign's Final Report, tools for citizen stormwater audits (typology mapping guide, neighbourhood audit walks), a masterclass on stormwater, and curated external resources on Bengaluru's water crisis. See also [[My_Publication]]. Keywords: stormwater resilience, Bengaluru, urban water, citizen science, water crisis, community campaign, final report

# Data

**Summary**: Notes on datasets, data sources, and data-related tools.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

---

- [TerraClimate](https://www.climatologylab.org/terraclimate.html): A dataset of monthly climate and climatic water balance for global terrestrial surfaces from 1950-present, at ~4-km (1/24th degree) spatial resolution. Combines high-resolution climate normals with time-varying reanalysis data; provides temperature, precipitation, wind speed, solar radiation, evapotranspiration, soil moisture, drought indices, and runoff, plus future projections (+2°C/+4°C warming scenarios) and a counterfactual no-anthropogenic-change scenario. Used for ecological/hydrological research such as streamflow modeling and climate change impact assessments. See [[Climate_Change]]. Keywords: climate data, water balance, global gridded dataset, drought index, evapotranspiration, climate projections

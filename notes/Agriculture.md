# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural mapping, field boundary delineation, and related geospatial applications.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Python package for delineating field boundaries. Combines seven complementary approaches — object detection, semantic segmentation, vision transformers, and embedding-based clustering — to extract agricultural field boundaries from satellite imagery. Handles the full workflow from satellite composite generation via Google Earth Engine through to vectorized field boundary polygons, supporting Landsat, Sentinel-2, NAIP, and SPOT sources, and filters out non-agricultural areas using land cover data. See also [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Python]]. Keywords: field boundary delineation, satellite imagery, Google Earth Engine, computer vision, agricultural mapping, Python
